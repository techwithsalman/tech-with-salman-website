Official Policy

PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: September 2026 • Effective Date: September 2026

Core Commitment: Tech With Salman operates with complete transparency. We never sell your personal or workspace data, never collect social network account passwords, and only access platform APIs with your explicit OAuth authorization to deliver requested scheduling and automation services.

1. WHO WE ARE

Tech With Salman ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Social Media OS platform and website (techwithsalman.online). This Privacy Policy explains how personal and workspace information is collected, utilized, and safeguarded when you interact with our website, use our software application, or contact our team.

For privacy-related inquiries, data rights requests, or compliance matters, contact our Data Protection Officer at: privacy@techwithsalman.online.

2. INFORMATION WE MAY COLLECT

We only collect information strictly required to authenticate your identity, provide high-performance workflow automation, and deliver social media management and scheduling features:

  • Account & Profile Information: Name, business name, registered email address, and authentication credentials (hashed passwords or Google/OAuth sign-in tokens).
  • Workspace & Social Channels: Workspace configuration settings, connected channel names/IDs, authorized social profiles, and team collaboration permissions.
  • Uploaded Content & Media Assets: Post text captions, scheduled publishing timestamps, tags, uploaded image files, video files, and graphic assets stored temporarily or during active scheduling queues.
  • Customer Support Inquiries: Communications, logs, or diagnostic information you voluntarily provide when seeking technical assistance.
  • Technical & Log Information: IP address, device type, browser characteristics, operating system, and system access logs utilized exclusively for security diagnostics, service integrity, and rate-limiting.

3. HOW WE USE INFORMATION

We process your information solely for lawful operational and service delivery purposes, including:

  • Authenticating authorized user sessions and securing multi-tenant workspace environments.
  • Scheduling, formatting, and publishing authorized social media posts to connected social platforms via official APIs upon your explicit instruction.
  • Generating workflow previews, content analytics summaries, and schedule status updates.
  • Providing technical support, diagnosing errors, and maintaining high platform availability.
  • Detecting and preventing fraud, unauthorized access, API abuse, and terms of service violations.

4. THIRD-PARTY PLATFORMS & OFFICIAL APIS

Social Media OS integrates with official third-party social media platform developer APIs, including Meta (Facebook Graph API, Instagram Graph API), TikTok API, Google (YouTube Data API), LinkedIn API, X (Twitter) API, Pinterest API, and Snapchat Marketing API.

  • OAuth Authorization: We connect to your social media accounts strictly via standard OAuth consent dialogs provided by the respective social network. You never provide your social media account passwords to Tech With Salman.
  • Scoped Permissions: We request only the minimal API permissions necessary to publish approved posts and read basic metric data (e.g. pages_manage_posts, instagram_content_publish, video.upload).
  • Adherence to Platform Terms: Our software strictly conforms to the Meta Developer Platform Policies, TikTok Developer Terms, Google API Services User Data Policy, and respective developer terms of all supported networks.
  • Zero Data Selling: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or broker your personal data, customer information, or media content to data brokers, advertisers, or third parties under any circumstances.
  • Independent Product Disclaimer: Social Media OS is an independent software product and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Meta, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Google, YouTube, or other third-party platforms.

5. DATA STORAGE & SECURITY SAFEGUARDS

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect stored information, including access controls and encrypted transport where supported:

  • Data in Transit: Data transmitted between your browser and our services is transmitted over encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS).
  • Access Controls: Access to workspace data and authorization credentials is restricted to authorized systems and personnel necessary to provide the services.
  • Operational Safeguards: We apply appropriate security practices and measures designed to protect user data from unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration.

6. USER DATA RIGHTS & DELETION

You retain full ownership and control over your personal information and uploaded media:

  • Access & Export: You may request an export of your stored workspace configuration and scheduled content records at any time.
  • Revocation of API Access: You can disconnect your social channels at any moment directly from the Social Media OS dashboard or via your native social network security settings.
  • Complete Data Purging: You have the right to request permanent deletion of your account and all associated media assets. Detailed step-by-step instructions are available on our dedicated Data Deletion Instructions Page.

7. COOKIES & LOCAL STORAGE

We use essential functional cookies and secure local storage mechanisms strictly for user session state maintenance, authentication security tokens, and user interface preferences (such as dark mode and workspace filters). We do not use third-party behavioral advertising trackers.

8. CHILDREN'S PRIVACY

Our website and applications are designed strictly for professionals, businesses, and creators aged 18 and older (or the applicable age of digital majority in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under 13.

9. POLICY UPDATES & CONTACT

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect software feature additions or regulatory revisions. When significant updates occur, we will update the "Last Updated" timestamp at the top of this document.
