PRIVACY & CONTROL

Your Accounts. Your Content.
Your Control.

Social Media OS is built with transparency and data protection at its core, utilizing official platform connection flows.

Secure Account Connections

Accounts are connected using standard OAuth authentication flows provided by each platform. We never ask for or store your account passwords.

Transparent Permissions

We only request permissions strictly necessary for publishing and content management features that you explicitly choose to configure.

Privacy Controls

Your media, captions, and account data remain your property. We do not sell your personal data or share it with third-party advertisers.

Data Deletion Support

Disconnect your accounts anytime and access clear public instructions for requesting deletion of applicable stored data.
